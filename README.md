## Business description (Stripe, directories…)

```
Coaching for makers and small online sellers, delivered online: one-on-one video sessions and small group sessions covering shop setup and listings, product photography and descriptions, pricing and understanding your real costs, and building repeat customers. Sellers follow an 8 to 12 week program with review of their shop and listings each week. We are not accountants or lawyers and give no tax, legal or platform-policy advice. Services are sold as month-to-month subscriptions from $39 to $189 per month, cancellable at any time. No physical products are sold or shipped. Site: thegrowthroom.shop
```
