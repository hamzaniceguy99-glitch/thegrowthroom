How we work

No agency, no guarantees, no insider access

The Growth Room isn't affiliated with Etsy, Shopify or any marketplace. We can't influence your ranking, restore a suspended account or get you featured, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling something.

We're also not accountants or lawyers. Sales tax, business registration, trademarks and platform policy disputes go to the right professional — we'll tell you when you've reached that point rather than guessing.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will this get me to the top of search?

No one can promise that. Better titles, photos and conversion help, and ranking depends on factors no coach controls.

My shop gets views but no sales.

Usually photos or price clarity rather than traffic. That's a good problem and it's generally fixable in a few weeks.

Do I need Shopify or is Etsy fine?

Depends on your volume and where your buyers come from. We'll go through it honestly — and we take no commission from either.

How much should I charge?

More than you think, usually. We build the number from your actual costs including your time, rather than from what other shops charge.

Can you help with sales tax?

No. That's an accountant's job and it varies by state. We'll flag when you need one.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Shop quiet?

Twenty minutes with your listings open and a straight assessment. Free.
