Why nobody's buying

Usually not the product. Usually the title nobody searches and the photo that hides the size.

How we work

Found, chosen, priced properly

Buyers type plain words. Shops that name products poetically disappear from search, and the fix is often a single afternoon of rewriting titles.

Then photos decide the sale. Window light, a plain background and one shot that shows scale remove most of the hesitation that stops someone adding to cart.

And pricing has to include your time. Makers who price from competitors end up working for a few dollars an hour, which is why a busy month can still feel like nothing.
