The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 12 weeks, worked on your own shop and your own numbers.

Program 01 · Found, then chosen

Shop & listings

Listings fail at two stages: being found and being chosen. We research the words buyers actually type, write titles around them without sounding robotic, and write descriptions that answer the questions that stop people buying — size, materials, shipping time, and whether it's a gift that will arrive on time.

  • 10 weeks
  • Have a shop
  • 4 hours a week
  • Your existing listings

Program 02 · The part that sells

Photography

Product photos do most of the selling and most makers' photos are dark and ambiguous about size. We set up window light and a cheap backdrop, shoot a standard set for every product, and add the scale and in-use shots that remove the biggest hesitation buyers have.

  • 8 weeks
  • A phone camera
  • 4 hours a week
  • A window and some card

Program 03 · Stop working for free

Pricing & costs

Most makers price by looking at other shops and then wonder why a good month leaves nothing. We count materials, your hours at a real rate, platform fees, packaging and shipping, then set prices from that — and work on raising existing prices without panic.

  • 6 weeks
  • Selling already
  • 3 hours a week
  • Your receipts

Program 04 · Cheaper than new ones

Repeat buyers

Chasing new traffic is expensive; selling again to someone who already liked your work isn't. We work on the unboxing and follow-up that produce reviews, a simple email list you'll actually write to, and craft fairs as both sales and list-building.
